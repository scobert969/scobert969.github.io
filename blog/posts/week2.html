I Can Put J-Pop Songs Here Because I Want To

6/9/2025

Ok I said that the next list would be shorter and it is. A few of these have been in the tank for a while, so I hope you enjoy them.

  • STEREOTYPE - STAYC
  • Mr. Vampire - ITZY
  • Real Girl - f5ve
  • TILT - Red Velvet - IRENE & SEULGI
  • Kiss My Troubles Away - TWICE
  • Secret - WJSN
  • Time for the moon night - GFRIEND
  • So Cynical (Badum) - LE SSERAFIM
  • You Are Blue, So Am I - BONNIE PINK

STEREOTYPE - STAYC

Yep we got another STAYC song. This is actually another one I saw Josh play on osu so I downloaded it for myself. I don't know what happened but now I've been playing it a lot. Not just that, but osu in general. You guys are lucky this didn't happen when we lived together.

Mr. Vampire - ITZY

A small bit of lore, Lia had to take a hiatus due to anxiety, and during that time ITZY released Born to Be as four members. It had this song on it, which I heard a few times. I liked the floaty and weird sound it had, but it did feel a bit incomplete without her. Then Lia came back and they released Goldas 5 again, featuring the rerecordings of the songs from Born to Be. I'm glad they did that.

Real Girl - f5ve

I don't know how online you guys are, but maybe you've heard of j-pop group f5ve somewhere. They're pretty funny and unserious, and so can their music sometimes, which is a whole lot of fun. And to top it all off their whole thing is like 90s/2000s themed. This song is probably my favorite from their debut album, SEQUENCE 01. I also played it on the way to the campsite.

TILT - Red Velvet (IRENE & SEULGI)

Since I'm writing about this, I feel kinda bad that Naughty isn't on the playlist anymore. Maybe it'll come back, because I do like the song, it just suffered from being overplayed in my car. At least we have TILT, the latest to come from this duo. Seulgi and Irene's voices are too good, well suited for a slow and intense song.

Kiss My Troubles Away - TWICE

Are you guys excited for the new TWICE album coming out? And the world tour? I am. In the meantime we still have Strategy and this song from it. A nice and upbeat song. I like the synths.

Secret - WJSN

Another banger I heard in the car. WJSN (or Cosmic Girls) used to be a 13 member group, until the Chinese members left after their contracts ended. This is one of their most popular songs, and yep, it deserves it. Actually, about the name, there was a tweet I saw a while ago about how people pronounce it. And not the Korean name, 우주소녀, but WJSN itself. There was a lot going on, but personally I landed on wee-juh-sen (wi-d͡ʒɘ-s̪e̞n (hopefully that's right)).

Time for the moon night - GFRIEND

So last time there was Sunrise, and now it's Time for the moon night. Nice little tune. Definitely night feeling. There's a lot more GFRIEND out there to talk about, but idk if it's to your guys' taste. Maybe I'll at least talk about Me Gustas Tu.

So Cynical (Badum) - LE SSERAFIM

This is another song that I played in the car on the way to the campsite, which Scott said he liked. I have to agree with him, this is like my favorite song from HOT. Fun fact, this song was the title screen music on Overwatch back when they did the second collab. I wish they did another music video for it, but oh well.

You Are Blue, So Am I - BONNIE PINK

When I bought a whole lot of MiniDiscs, there was a blue disc looked super cool. I was blown away when I first listened to it that I let the whole thing play. But when it came time to archive and erase all the discs I couldn't bring myself to erase that one. Eventually I did, but I messed it up, and it got corrupted. Fortunately I was still able to identify the album and there I was introduced to BONNIE PINK. This song really gives me that 2000s nostalgia. Maybe it's the guitar.

Yep, I kinda ran out of songs to talk about, and I got lazy with the website. Please let me know if you actually listened to these songs. Please like at least one of them.
